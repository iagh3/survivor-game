How To Play
🎯 Mission Loop
Purge enemies → Collect DATA orbs → Sync Level up → Choose upgrade → Survive escalating waves.
🕹 Controls
PC: WASD / Arrow keys — move Hold LMB toward cursor — move to cursor Mouse cursor sets attack priority direction Mobile: Left joystick — move Attack is always automatic in range
☣ Enemy Types
Fast (triangle) — quick, low HP, attacks on contact Tank (hexagon) — slow, high HP, heavy damage Ranged (diamond) — keeps distance, shoots projectiles
⬆ Upgrade Colors
🔴 RED — Assault: core damage boost 🔵 BLUE — Mobility / Ultimate: speed & overheat 💜 VIOLET — Tempo / Sustain: attack speed & lifesteal 🟢 GREEN — Defense: +HP and healing 🔵 CYAN — Control: wider attack range
⚡ Combat Systems
OVERHEAT — fills with every kill. At max: auto-blast destroys all nearby enemies and resets. LOW INTEGRITY — below 25% HP the screen enters panic mode. Use BLOOD LINK upgrade to restore HP on kills. OVERHEAT DISCHARGE — the best panic button. Upgrade NOVA LATTICE to charge it faster.
